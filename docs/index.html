Built for the AI coding workflow

Stop screenshotting. Stop copying CSS selectors by hand. Click elements, type notes, paste a clean payload into your AI agent, ship the PR.

Stable selectors

Every annotation captures tag, ID, classes, XPath, the URL, and a snippet of the clicked element's text, so your AI tool can locate the element without ambiguity.

Unlimited annotations

Annotations are grouped by page URL in the popup. Add as many as you want. Free tier has no limits on the number of notes you can take.

Inline editing

Edit any note directly in the popup. Auto-expanding textboxes save as you type. Per-row delete moves notes to History instead of erasing them.

Cut as Markdown

Hit Cut All to copy a clean Markdown bundle and clear the list. Right-click Cut All to copy without clearing. The format is tuned for paste-into-AI use.

History view

Deleted annotations are kept with timestamps. Restore any entry with one click. Search works across both the live list and the history panel.

Search everything

Hit Cmd or Ctrl + F inside the popup to search both your active annotations and your history at the same time.

Click to navigate

Click a pink selector or a blue URL group to jump straight to the annotated element on the page. The annotation panel opens automatically.

Undo, not confirm

Clear All shows a 5 second undo banner instead of a confirmation dialog. Recover from mistakes without breaking flow.

Auto backup

Optional backup to chrome.storage.sync plus a local in-browser snapshot every 15 minutes. No download prompts, no servers, fully opt-in.

How it works

  1. 1

    Open the page you are reviewing

  2. 2

    Alt + Right-Click any element

    Type a note, press Esc or click outside to save. Empty notes are auto-discarded.

  3. 3

    Review and edit in the popup

    Annotations are grouped by page URL. Edit notes inline, delete or restore from History, and search across everything.

  4. 4

    Cut as Markdown, paste into your AI tool

    Hit Cut All. A clean Markdown payload lands on your clipboard. Paste it into Cursor, Claude, ChatGPT, Copilot, or v0. The agent ships the PR.

Frequently asked questions

Does the extension send my data anywhere?

No. There is no server, no analytics, and no telemetry. Annotations live in chrome.storage.local on your device. The optional Auto Backup mirrors a compressed bundle into chrome.storage.sync, which Google encrypts in transit and at rest.

What does it read from the page?

Only when you actively annotate. It computes a stable CSS selector and captures up to 240 characters of the clicked element's text. It does not read passwords, form values, cookies, or storage.

Is Premium a subscription?

No. Premium is a one-time $9.99 purchase. Your license key works forever, no recurring charges.

How do I activate Premium?

Click Get Premium and complete the $9.99 Stripe checkout. You'll be redirected to a success page that shows your license key with a copy button. The same key is also in your Stripe receipt email under "License key:". Open the extension popup, click the gear icon, paste your key in the Premium section, and click Activate.

Which AI tools does it work with?

Any AI tool that reads Markdown. The export format is tuned for Cursor, Claude, ChatGPT, GitHub Copilot Chat, and v0, but works with any model.

Are there any limitations?

Yes. Annotations live in the top frame, so elements inside iframes attach to the iframe container. Closed shadow roots cannot be pierced. The extension runs on http and https URLs only, not file:// or chrome:// pages.
